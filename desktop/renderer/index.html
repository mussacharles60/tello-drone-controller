Getting Started
Important — network: your computer must join the drone's own Wi-Fi network — it appears in your Wi-Fi list as something like TELLO-XXXXXX. The Tello does not connect to your home router; it creates its own hotspot, and there is no internet access while you're joined to it.
  1. Power on the Tello.
  2. On your computer, open Wi-Fi settings and connect to TELLO-XXXXXX (no password by default).
  3. Come back here and click Connect in the top bar. Once connected you'll see a green dot and the battery percentage.
  4. Takeoff / Land — the big toggle button, or press Space.
  5. Move — the D-pad, or keyboard W A S D to move, Q E to rotate, ↑ ↓ for altitude. Distance/angle per press are set by the Trim sliders. Keyboard flight controls only work once you're airborne.
  6. Flip — four buttons, enabled only mid-flight.
  7. Start Video to see the live feed; Record saves an .mp4 to your Videos/TelloPro folder.
  8. Emergency Stop cuts the motors instantly — only for when something's actually gone wrong, since the drone will drop.
  9. Disconnect only becomes available once landed, as a safety measure.
